About SVP

Built by creators, for creators. Learn about the mission behind Al Nostro Social Video Pipeline.

Our Mission

Al Nostro Social Video Pipeline (SVP) was born out of a simple need: managing content across multiple platforms without sacrificing privacy, control, or time. Our team recognized that cloud-based solutions were often slow, expensive, and required uploading massive raw files just to get a few simple clips.

We built SVP as a local-first desktop application to give creators the power of automated transcribing, smart 9:16 portrait tracking, and direct API scheduling—all from their own machine.

Why Local?

In an era of centralized cloud services, we believe your videos, your credentials, and your processing power should remain yours. By leveraging the power of modern desktop hardware, SVP ensures that your unreleased content stays completely secure until the moment it's published.

Who We Are

Al Nostro is a small, dedicated team of software engineers and content creators. We build tools that solve our own workflow bottlenecks, and we share them with the world to help other creators scale their reach without scaling their stress.
